Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. How Your Text, Voice, and Images Are Processed

When you use Nova Translator to translate text, spoken audio, or camera images, your input is sent securely to our translation service to generate the translated output. We do not require you to create an account or submit personal identity details to use the translator.

2. What Is Stored in Your Browser

Nova Translator stores your recent translation history and saved phrases only inside your own browser's local storage if you use the History or Saved features. You can view or clear this local history at any time from your browser or the History page.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.

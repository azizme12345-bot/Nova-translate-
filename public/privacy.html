Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains simply how LAFZ Translation handles your text, voice, and images. No sign-in or account is required.

Text and Image Translation

Typed text you translate is sent through our server to Google Gemini to translate it. Photos uploaded in Camera OCR are also sent through our server to Google Gemini to read and translate the text.

Voice Input and Listen

Microphone input uses your browser's speech recognition. When you use Chrome, speech recognition is handled by Google's speech service. The Listen buttons use your browser and device's built-in speech synthesis.

Browser Storage

Your recent translation history, saved items, and preferences stay only in your browser's localStorage on your device. You can delete them anytime with the Clear button or your browser settings.

Sensitive Information

Please do not enter passwords, bank details, or private ID documents.

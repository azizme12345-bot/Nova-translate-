About LAFZ

LAFZ Translation is built around one simple idea: understanding should feel easy. The name “LAFZ” means “word”, and every word can help people share ideas, learn something new and connect across language differences.

Our approach

We focus on a clear layout, readable text and practical tools. The translator keeps the original text and translated result side by side on larger screens, while adapting into a comfortable stacked layout on mobile devices. Helpful actions such as copy, listen and saved history are kept close to the text they belong to.

Keep improving

Translation quality depends on the translation service connected to this website. We aim to make that process straightforward and to show clear messages when a service is unavailable, rather than displaying made-up results. LAFZ is an independent translation interface and is not affiliated with Google, Microsoft or any other translation provider.

Privacy

Your privacy matters. This website stores saved translation history in your browser's local storage so you can reopen it on the same device and browser. This history is not automatically synced between devices.

Text you submit

When a translation service is connected, the text you submit may be sent to that service's server to produce a translation. The handling of submitted text depends on the provider and the website's server configuration. Do not submit passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information.

Browser features

Voice input and spoken output, when available, use browser or device speech features. Their availability and data handling may vary by browser and operating system.

Your choices

You can delete individual saved translations or clear your entire local history from the Home page. Clearing browser site data may also remove saved history. This page should be updated with the exact service provider, retention policy and contact details before the website is publicly launched.

About Lafz Translator

Lafz Translator is a fast, clean web application built for everyday translation between English, Urdu, and more than 100 other languages.

How to use Lafz Translator

  1. Type or speak: Enter your text in the box, tap the microphone to speak, or scan printed text with your camera.
  2. Choose the language: Select your source and target languages, such as English and Urdu, or swap them with one tap.
  3. Read or listen to the translation: View the result right away, copy it to your clipboard, or tap the speaker icon to hear it aloud.

Why Lafz Translator

  • Fast results: Translations appear quickly so you can keep conversations moving without waiting.
  • Simple layout: Clean controls for text, voice, and camera make it easy to use on any phone or computer.
  • Privacy in the browser: Your translation history stays in your own browser storage, and you can clear it anytime.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, Lafz Translator is free to use for text, voice, and camera translation in your browser.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, it works on Android and iPhone browsers and can be added to your home screen like an app.

Does it need an account?

No account or sign-in is needed. Open the page and start translating right away.

What LAFZ Translation Is

LAFZ Translation is a free, straightforward web interface built to translate everyday words, sentences, and short paragraphs across English, Urdu, and over 100 global languages. All text translations are performed on our server through the connected translation service, so there are no accounts to register and no downloads required to begin. Whether you are reading an article, writing a message, or checking an unfamiliar phrase, everything loads in one clean screen. LAFZ Translation is an independent interface and is not affiliated with Google or any other translation provider.

How to Translate Text Step by Step

Start by picking your source language in the left dropdown menu and your target language in the right dropdown menu, or tap Swap to reverse both directions at once. Next, type your text or tap Paste to insert copied text into the source box. Click Translate or pause briefly for the translated wording to appear in the output box, where you can copy, save, or clear your text in a single tap.

Voice Input, Spoken Output, and Camera OCR

If you prefer speaking over typing, tap Speak and allow microphone access in your browser. When voice input records audio from your microphone, that audio is sent to the server to convert your speech into text for translation. Tap Listen next to the result to hear spoken output powered by your browser and device speech features. To translate printed menus, street signs, receipts, or documents, open Camera OCR to pick an image and send it to the server so its text can be extracted and translated.

Language Choices, History, and Helpful Tips

Right-to-left scripts such as Urdu, Arabic, and Punjabi align properly on the page so text remains easy to read. Your recent translations are saved locally in your browser's localStorage and are not synced between devices, and you can search or clear entries whenever you want. For better results, use clear spelling, standard punctuation, and short complete sentences rather than slang or heavy abbreviations. Because translations are generated automatically by a machine, they may not always be perfect, so please check important wording carefully before sharing it.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains in plain, honest language how LAFZ Translation handles the text, voice audio, and images you translate, and what information stays inside your own web browser.

How Text and Images Are Sent to the Server

No user account, sign-in, or personal profile is needed to use LAFZ Translation. When you enter written text and request a translation, that text is sent to our server and processed through the connected translation service so the result can be returned to your screen. When you choose Camera OCR, the selected image is sent to the server to extract printed text and translate it.

How Voice Input and Spoken Output Work

Microphone access starts only when you press Speak and approve permission in your browser. When voice input records audio from your microphone, that audio is sent to the server to convert speech into text before translation. When you press Listen, spoken playback uses your browser and device speech features so you can hear how translated text sounds.

What Is Stored in Your Browser

Your recent translation history, saved items, and basic interface preferences are stored locally inside your browser's localStorage. This browser storage stays strictly inside the current browser on your phone, tablet, or computer and is not synced across different devices or uploaded to build a user profile. Keeping history local helps you revisit recent translations quickly while keeping control on your own device.

Information You Should Not Submit

Please never type, speak, or upload passwords, bank account numbers, payment card details, government identity documents, private medical records, or other sensitive personal information. Any text, audio, or image submitted for translation travels over the network to the server for processing.

How to Clear History and Independent Notice

You can remove individual translations or clear your entire history at any time using the Clear button on the Home page or History page, or by clearing site storage inside your browser settings. Once cleared, those saved entries are removed from that browser. LAFZ Translation is an independent interface and is not affiliated with Google or any other translation provider.
